**Transition from Textbook:** This document recommends LinkedIn Learning video courses to replace/supplement "Principles of Incident Response & Disaster Recovery" (Whitman & Mattord, 3rd Ed.) for BIT360 Week 1.

**Week 1 Learning Objectives:**

## Comparison: Textbook vs. LinkedIn Learning

| Aspect | Textbook (Whitman & Mattord) | LinkedIn Learning Videos |
|---|---|---|
| **Format** | Reading + self-study | Video instruction + expert presenters |
| **Engagement** | Text-based (passive) | Visual + auditory (interactive) |
| **Flexibility** | Linear progression | Self-paced, watchable on-demand |
| **Expert Context** | Academic theory | Industry practitioner perspectives |
| **Case Studies** | Included in text | Embedded in video explanations |
| **Compliance Focus** | General frameworks | Governance/compliance-focused option |
| **Cost** | ~$100-200 per textbook | Institutional LinkedIn Learning subscription |
| **Updates** | Edition-based (slow) | Regularly updated courses |
