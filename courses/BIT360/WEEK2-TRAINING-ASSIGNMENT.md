## Learning Objectives

After completing this training, students will:
1. Understand strategic risk management and how to think about pressing risks of the 21st century
2. Analyze organizational assets and vulnerabilities in the context of emerging threats
3. Evaluate business continuity implications of globalization, technology disruption, and supply chain risk
4. Apply strategic risk thinking to contingency planning and organizational readiness
5. Prepare for Week 2 assignments: organizational diagramming and case study analysis

## Training Content Topics

- Strategic approach to identifying and managing organizational risks
- Emerging threats: climate change, supply chain disruption, technology disruption
- Cryptocurrency risk and financial risk management
- Asset protection strategies for critical business functions
- Organizational vulnerability assessment and readiness planning
- Life insurance, insurance strategies, and risk transfer approaches
- Building organizational resilience through strategic risk management
