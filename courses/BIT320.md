## Course Structure & Pedagogy (Sequential Language Mastery)

5-week, 2-credit course (20 contact hours, ~40 homework hours). Students solve the **same core log analysis problem** across three languages in Weeks 1-3 so each week is a language reset rather than a problem-complexity leap, then compare all three in Week 4 and apply their chosen language to Windows Event Logs in Week 5.

- **Infrastructure:** Existing Hyper-V VMs (from BIT221/PRO221) + WSL2 on Windows (`~/bit320_scripts` vs Windows path `/mnt/c/Users/...`).
- **Teaching principle:** Teach the language, do not answer the graded questions in assignments or student pages. Reference solutions and grading tables live exclusively in unpublished instructor answer-key pages and [`courses/BIT320/`](.).
