## Learning Objectives

After completing this training, students will:
1. Understand risk identification techniques and threat categorization
2. Analyze likelihood and impact scales for risk evaluation
3. Apply risk assessment frameworks to prioritize organizational threats
4. Evaluate mitigation strategies (reduce, accept, transfer, avoid)
5. Make data-driven risk prioritization decisions for Week 1B assignment

---

## Training Content Topics

- Risk identification and threat categorization techniques
- Likelihood and impact scales for risk assessment
- Quantitative vs. qualitative risk evaluation approaches
- Risk matrix creation and interpretation
- Risk mitigation strategy selection (reduce, accept, transfer, avoid)
- Risk prioritization and decision-making frameworks
- Organizational context and business alignment in risk decisions

## Implementation Notes

### For Instructors

**Before Week 1 Starts:**
1. Verify Neumont's LinkedIn Learning institutional subscription is active
2. Confirm the course "IT and Cybersecurity Risk Management Essential Training" is accessible
3. Review assignment URL: https://lms.neumont.edu/courses/3644702/assignments/40349945

5. Publish assignment before sharing with students (currently unpublished)

**During Week 1:**
1. Announce training assignment in syllabus or welcome email
2. Emphasize: "Watch this training before working on Assignment 1B (Hyper-V + Risk Assessment)"
3. Provide LinkedIn Learning access instructions if students haven't used it before
4. Be available for questions on course access

**Grading Workflow:**
1. Check submission for screenshot and completeness
2. Award full 80 pts for clear completion proof
3. Minor deductions (10-15 pts) for image quality or account visibility issues
4. Zero points if no proof of completion or screenshot unreadable

### For Students

**Timeline Recommendation:**
- **Before Week 1 Class Starts:** Watch the training video (1h 38m) at your own pace
- **During Week 1:** Work on assignments 1A (SysInternals), 1B (Hyper-V), and Training 1A (submit screenshot)
- **By End of Week 1:** Submit all three assignments

**Common Questions:**

**Q: Do I need to take notes?**  
A: You don't need to submit notes, only a screenshot of completion. However, taking notes helps you apply risk concepts to Assignment 1B (threat modeling and risk assessment).

**Q: Can I watch at 1.5x or 2x speed?**  
A: Yes! LinkedIn Learning allows video speed adjustment. Watching at 1.5x is common and reduces viewing time to ~1 hour.

**Q: What if I can't access LinkedIn Learning?**  
A: Contact Neumont IT support with your issue. They can verify your institutional account access. Mention you need "IT and Cybersecurity Risk Management Essential Training" from LinkedIn Learning.

**Q: What if I forget to take a screenshot?**  
A: You can retake the course (LinkedIn Learning tracks completion) and submit a new screenshot at any time before the deadline.

**Q: Do I need to complete the full course or can I skip sections?**  
A: Complete the entire course for full credit. LinkedIn Learning tracks completion percentage. You need 100% shown in your screenshot for full 80 points.
