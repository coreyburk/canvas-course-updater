## How This Compares to the Textbook

**Textbook Approach:**
- Read Module 1 chapters (Risk Management, Contingency Planning, Policies)
- Self-study; concepts explained in prose
- Passive learning (reading)
- Updated every 5-7 years

**LinkedIn Learning Approach:**
- Watch expert instructors explain concepts live
- Visual demonstrations and slide presentations
- Active learning (video + note-taking)
- Updated quarterly; current industry practices
- Practitioner perspectives (not just academic theory)
- Can watch at your pace, rewind, watch again as needed
