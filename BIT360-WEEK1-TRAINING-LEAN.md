# BIT360 Week 1: Lean Training Approach
## Replace Textbook Reading with Focused LinkedIn Learning Video

**Guiding Principle:** Students already have comprehensive written guidance (3,500-4,500 word student resource pages). Training videos should be minimal, focused, and directly support the assignments — not duplicate what's already published.

---

## Week 1 Time Budget Analysis

**Total Class Time:** 5:20 per week (320 minutes)
- 4 days × 80 minutes/day
- Split among: instruction, lab setup, demo, Q&A, office hours

**Assignment Time (Non-Trivial):**
- **1A: SysInternals** — Process Explorer, TCPView, Event Log analysis (2-3 hours)
- **1B: Hyper-V + 4 VMs** — Installation, configuration, snapshots, network setup (3-5 hours)
- **Total Assignment Time:** 5-8 hours minimum

**Available Training Time:** 2-3 hours maximum (doesn't exceed 25% of weekly load)

---

## Revised Recommendation: ONE Essential Video

### **Primary Course (Replace Textbook Module 1)**

**[IT and Cybersecurity Risk Management Essential Training](https://www.linkedin.com/learning/it-and-cybersecurity-risk-management-essential-training)**
- **Duration:** 1h 38m (fits in 2-hour budget)
- **Content Coverage:**
  - Risk identification and assessment
  - Likelihood × impact evaluation
  - Mitigation strategies and decision-making
  - Risk prioritization frameworks
  
- **Why This One?**
  - Directly supports assignment (1B requires threat modeling and risk assessment)
  - Textbook Module 1 focuses heavily on "Risk Management"
  - Practitioner perspective (vs. academic theory)
  - Self-contained; doesn't require prerequisites
  - Students can watch asynchronously before week starts

- **Assignment Alignment:**
  - Risk Matrix (likelihood × impact) → used in 1B threat assessment
  - Mitigation decision trees → needed for assignment recommendations
  - Risk language → helps students articulate findings in reports

## Week 1 Learning Flow (Optimized)

### **Before Week 1 (Self-Paced)**
1. **Watch:** IT and Cybersecurity Risk Management (1h 38m)
   - Understand risk assessment concepts and frameworks
   
### **Week 1 (In Class + Async)**
2. **Read:** Week 1 Student Page 1.1 (InfoSec Fundamentals & Risk Assessment) (1-1.5 hours)
   - Apply risk management concepts to ACIO framework
   - Learn CIA triad and threat modeling
   
3. **Read:** Week 1 Student Page 1.2 (SysInternals Tools) (1-1.5 hours)
   - Study tool workflows and examples
   
4. **Read:** Week 1 Student Page 1.3 (Lab Setup) (1 hour)
   - Reference during Hyper-V setup

5. **Do Assignments:**
   - **1A: SysInternals** — Apply tools from Page 1.2 (2-3 hours)
   - **1B: Hyper-V Setup** — Use Page 1.3 + risk assessment from Page 1.1 (3-5 hours)

## Why This Approach Works

| Metric | Overloaded Approach | Lean Approach |
|--------|---|---|
| **Training Video Time** | 4.5-5 hours | 1h 38m |
| **Reading Time** | 2-3 hours (textbook) | 3-4 hours (interactive Canvas pages) |
| **Assignment Time** | 5-8 hours | 5-8 hours |
| **Total Student Workload** | 11.5-16 hours | 9.5-13.5 hours |
| **Resource Redundancy** | Video + textbook + Canvas pages | Video + Canvas pages only |
| **Cognitive Load** | High (overwhelming) | Moderate (manageable) |
| **Engagement** | Lower (passive reading) | Higher (interactive Canvas + video + hands-on) |
